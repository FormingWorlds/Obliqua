Forcing Frequency

Given the fact that the tidal forcing magnitude decreases exponentially with harmonic order, we may limit the calculation to only the lowest harmonic degree $n = 2$. Generally, in the limit of $R_p \ll a$, it suffices to only consider the quadrupolar harmonic ($n = 2$). Nevertheless, Obliqua can also determine higher degree contributions. As of now, we are considering a coplanar geometry. Hence, terms with $m = 1$ associated with obliquity tides vanish. The Fourier modes of the second harmonic have frequencies given by

\[\sigma = m\Omega - k n_{\mathrm{orb}},\]

where $\Omega$ is spin rate and $n_{\mathrm{orb}}$ orbital mean motion, and for integer values of order $-2 \leq m \leq 2$ and harmonic $-\infty \leq k \leq \infty$. In our formalism tides are occuring over a large time interval, a time step $\Delta t$. As such, we must account for tidal excitations that occur over a wide range of frequencies. We calculate the imaginary part of the $n$th harmonic degree ($k_n$) Love number ($\Im[k_{n}(\sigma)]$) for all relevant harmonic frequencies. The Hansen coefficients

\[X^{-(n+1), m}_k(e) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_0^{2\pi} \left(\frac{r}{a}\right)^{-(n+1)} e^{imv}\, e^{-ikM}\,dM ,\]

with $v$ the true and $M$ the mean anomaly, are computed by FFT over $M$ (Kepler's equation solved by a bracketed Newton iteration, so any $e < 1$ is supported). In the adaptive spectrum the set $K = [k_\mathrm{lo}, k_\mathrm{hi}]$ of harmonics is chosen from this spectrum: with weights $w_k = (1 + |k|) X_k^2$, $K$ is the smallest range that leaves less than $\varepsilon\sum_k w_k$ outside on each side, where $\varepsilon$ is the config value hansen_tail_tol (default $10^{-6}$). $X_k^2$ measures the tidal potential of mode $k$ and $|k| X_k^2$ its heating under a frequency-independent phase lag. A configured s_min or s_max replaces the matching end. For $m = 0$ the spectrum is symmetric in $k$ and only $k \geq 0$ is kept. The weight is explained under "k-range" on the main loop page.

Function Documentation

Obliqua.Hansen.hansen_modes — Function
hansen_modes(e, n, m; tail_tol=1e-6)

Mode range of the degree-n, order-m tide at eccentricity e, with coefficients Xs^{-(n+1),m}(e) from `hansenspectrum. The contiguous range [s_min, s_max] is the smallest one that leaves less thantailtol` of the weight sums (1 + |s|) Xs^2 outside on each side: Xs^2 weights the tidal potential and |s| Xs^2 the dissipation of a response with a frequency-independent phase lag. For m = 0 only s >= 0 is returned (X-s = X_s; the caller counts the mirrored modes twice).

Returns (s, X) with s = smin:smax.

Obliqua.Hansen.hansen_spectrum — Function
hansen_spectrum(n, m, e; N=nothing, alias_tol=1e-13)

Hansen coefficients Xs^{n,m}(e) = (1/2π) ∫ (r/a)^n exp(i m v) exp(-i s M) dM for all s in -N/2 : N/2-1, by FFT over the mean anomaly M with `keplersafe. WithoutNthe length starts at 2^p >= 64 (1 - e)^(-3/2) (at least 2^12) and doubles until the share of the spectral energy in |s| > N/4 is belowalias_tol` (at most 2^26 points). Returns (s, X, N).

Obliqua.Hansen.kepler_safe — Function
kepler_safe(M, e)

Eccentric anomaly E in [0, 2π) with E - e sin E = M for M in [0, 2π), 0 <= e < 1. Newton iteration kept inside the bracket [M, M + e] (for M <= π; the other half by symmetry), with bisection whenever a step leaves the bracket, so the iteration converges for every e < 1.
